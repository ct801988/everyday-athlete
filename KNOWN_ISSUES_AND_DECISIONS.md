- **No phone-accessible link or distribution yet.** Loopback demo runs on the development computer. Owner must approve an appropriate stable HTTPS distribution method before iPhone/family access or enrollment. No paid or public hosting is implied.
- **Local data-loss exposure.** Clearing, private browsing, eviction or device loss can destroy history and internal rollback datasets. The app cannot verify that downloaded backup files were retained. Backups are unencrypted; anyone with device/profile/file access may read them. There is no same-browser-profile security isolation.
- **Pilot scale, not a high-volume engine.** Each dataset is atomically stored as an aggregate, with audit snapshots. Size/node/field limits eventually refuse further writes; no history is silently pruned. Export well before capacity. A later capacity/archiving design needs approval; no unlimited-record claim.
- **Restore is whole-workspace replacement with preview, not merge.** Old snapshots can replace the active dataset after confirmation/current export. Revisions and pinned identities guard contradictions; no cross-device synchronization. Retained inactive datasets consume space and are not automatically deleted.
- **Offline depends on prior caching and a compatible stable origin/browser.** First offline visit, unsupported file:// launch and origin switching do not work as a portable installation. Timers preserve deadlines, not guaranteed background alarms; device clock changes can affect displayed countdown. Full OS power-loss durability cannot be guaranteed by browser APIs.
- **Cloud still unverified.** Supabase Auth/JWT/Storage/native restoration and account linkage remain deferred, not waived. Synthetic lab auth is isolated, never production authentication. Future cloud data requires separate approval and native verification.
