# Exercise-science evidence framework
Version 1.0 • DEC-006 design; research checked 17 October 2026.

## What this review establishes
This is a targeted product-oriented literature review, not a registered systematic review, clinical guideline or claim of comprehensive coverage. Sources prioritize organizations, original journals and PubMed/PMC records; bibliography and access depth are recorded in 14. Search-engine summaries do not certify full-text appraisal. The 2026 ACSM stand is verified, not a remembered or hypothetical update. Reviewer must inspect complete methods, applicability and licensing before any coaching rule is published.

Broad evidence can justify resistance training and some programming choices. It cannot certify every threshold, exact eight-week routine, individual load jump, pain response, fixed deload cadence or app efficacy. Program parameters and R01–R11 are transparent, conservative design proposals. Do not use “scientifically optimal,” “injury-proof” or equivalent claims.

## Search and appraisal procedure
Search concepts used: ACSM 2026 resistance prescription; NSCA resistance training position statements; WHO activity guidelines; resistance training load/sets/frequency network meta-analysis; proximity to failure/RIR; rest intervals; range of motion; attentional focus; adherence; IOC load/recovery; detraining. Primary records were retrieved on 17 October 2026. Exact starting queries and access limitations are summarized in 14. No paid database subscription or proprietary content ingestion occurred.

Future repeatable search: curator records database, exact query, date window, hit count and screened inclusions/exclusions. Include adult population relevant to intended user, defined training intervention, interpretable outcomes and methods; prefer guidelines/position stands and syntheses, then informative controlled trials. Exclude unsupported social claims, undisclosed commercial coaching promises and sources without verifiable identity. Preprints/elite practice may enter hypothesis queue only. Record duplicate cohorts/review overlap so the same trial is not counted as independent confirmation across umbrella reviews.

Appraise risk of bias, certainty/precision, heterogeneity, publication bias, measurement validity, baseline training status, duration, sex/age representation and real-world adherence. Distinguish strength test specificity, muscle-size measurements, functional outcomes and user experience. Mechanistic acute findings do not automatically predict longitudinal adaptation. A trial mean is not an individualized response forecast. Reviewer selects appropriate appraisal tool (e.g. AMSTAR2/ROB2/GRADE principles) and documents findings; none of those formal ratings has been performed here.

## Confidence vocabulary
- High applicability confidence: convergent evidence in target-like populations for a broad principle, with limitations recorded.
- Moderate: useful evidence with heterogeneity, short trials, indirectness or measurement limitations.
- Low/indirect: small samples, elite or clinical mismatch, observational associations or mechanisms.
- Practice/design choice: parameter needed for software/programming, no claim of direct experimental validation.
These are provisional product labels, not completed formal GRADE ratings. Source type is separate from confidence: a position stand is not automatically high certainty for every subclaim.

## Evidence-to-rule translation

| Domain | Evidence input | Permitted design use | Prohibited extrapolation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strength/hypertrophy | E01/E02 | Goal-specific reviewed sets/ranges and progression principles | Universal optimal routine or guaranteed gain |
| Effort/RIR | E04/E05/E06 | User-reported effort as one gate, explain uncertainty | Infer unknown RIR, mandate failure, infer safe form |
| Activity/general health | E03 | Explain overall activity goal vs limited program dose | Promise scheduled walk alone meets all health guidance |
| Recovery/load | E11 and programming review | Consider context, hold after interruptions, optional lighter work | Diagnostic overtraining score or validated injury-risk ratio |
| Biomechanics/motor learning | E08/E09 | Reviewed range/cues/media, simple understandable instructions | Prove anatomy or skill from logs/photos alone |
| Adherence | E10 | Test burden, choice and clarity with users | Claim reminder design causes adherence from association |
| Elite performance | E11 and future qualified practice review | Generate bounded research questions | Import competition workloads into beginner programs |

## Register schema and governance
Each source/version stores evidence_id, citation/authors/title/year/journal, DOI/URL/PMID, publication and search cutoff dates, retrieved/review_due dates, study design/population/training status, intervention/comparator/duration, outcomes, authored finding, uncertainty/contraindication applicability, access depth, license/access restrictions, retraction/correction status, confidence rationale, linked rule/program IDs, curator, reviewer and approval status. Outcome-specific findings have separate IDs when confidence differs. No diagnosis list fabricated from a study not addressing contraindications.

Lifecycle: discovered → metadata verified → full-text appraised → rights checked → interpretation reviewed → approved for specific use → published version → superseded/withdrawn. Current registry entries are candidate evidence; no named human reviewer has signed them. Clinical reviewer checks screening/referral copy. Owner approves product scope, not scientific truth. Rule publication requires test fixtures, reviewed rationale, content hashes and explicit reviewer/date. Record disagreement and hold release for unresolved safety questions.

Review cadence proposal: quarterly plus immediate triage for guideline updates, retractions, safety corrections or repeated user confusion. Curator submits change diff, applicability assessment and tests; reviewer approves; staged pilot release retains old run history. New publications never automatically change active prescriptions. If evidence is withdrawn, disable affected future recommendations or use an approved conservative fallback; preserve provenance and communicate material safety changes.

## Lawful acquisition
Store bibliographic metadata/links and independently authored summaries by default. Check article-level license before storing or redistributing full text/figures, even for PMC or free pages. WHO/NSCA/ACSM publications and textbooks have their own terms. Do not ingest paid certification curricula, purchased textbooks or copyrighted exercise images without rights. References may be read for appraisal without granting redistribution permission. No full articles, figures or paid course extracts are bundled here.
