# Everyday Athlete milestone handoff standard
Standing owner instruction •17 October2026 • Applies at end of every assignment.

Deliver MILESTONE_HANDOFF.md with these six sections:
1. Approved scope vs completed work: requirement/work-package IDs; partial/deferred/unstarted items identified.
2. Actual build/test results: commands, environment/versions, pass/fail/skip counts and failures with reproduction; distinguish proposed tests, emulation and real-device checks.
3. Files created/modified: manifest plus purposes; link the exact review package/release version.
4. Outstanding bugs/risks/decisions: severity, impact, workaround, owner/dependency and unresolved scientific/UX assumptions; no invented “none” from untested code.
5. Recommended next assignment: bounded objective/scope/exclusions/tests/deliverables and dependencies, explicitly not started.
6. Approval-required actions: major scope changes, cost, production deployment, irreversible operations and next-major-assignment authorization.

Package source/deliverables, README, results, decisions/changelog and the handoff for the Everyday Athlete strategy conversation. Keep current decision register and product requirements authoritative. Link immutable versioned artifacts and retain historical decisions with clear supersession. Verify required files and readable ZIP; future code ZIPs also verify installation/build behavior. Include credential/cost limitations and rollback notes.

Stop at milestone delivery. Passing tests, prior scope approval or a recommended next step does not authorize the next major assignment. Owner approval is explicit. Routine reversible choices inside an authorized assignment do not require repeated questions; missing critical inputs and material scope choices do.
