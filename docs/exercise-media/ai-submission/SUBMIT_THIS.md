# Quick submission checklist for standard ChatGPT
Ask the design conversation to prepare EA_media-pilot_submission_v001.zip using IMAGE_SUBMISSION_SPEC.md and submission-manifest.example.json.

Send:
1. Individual original PNG/JPG/WebP frames: three bench images and the reviewed hip CAR count (initially eight). Use the manifest's exact exercise/variant/version/order filenames. No collage or screenshots.
2. One short caption per frame; select a recognizable thumbnail frame. Identify side, direction, support, equipment and any intentional camera changes.
3. Generator/tool and date; model if known; prompts/edit history; original export identifiers; reference-input sources/permissions; applicable commercial-use terms/evidence. Unknown information stays pending.
4. Your exact visual selection/approval of these versions. List qualified technique and terms review as pending unless actually completed; don't let the assistant invent reviewer credentials, licensing or medical validation.
5. README describing changes or missing items. Include provenance and review-status text files. Work can fill actual dimensions/hashes and complete the mapping manifest from your supplied information; you do not need to calculate hashes yourself.

Preferred master: 2048×1536 / 4:3. Native 1536×1024 exports are acceptable for inspection; preserve the original and don't invent detail by upscaling. Work will optimize approved display assets and thumbnails later.

The ZIP may be submitted for review with pending fields. It is not production approval or permission to publish. No photography/stock-service budget is approved. Storyboards remain planning references; do not generate additional library exercises or alter generic workout-history identities.
