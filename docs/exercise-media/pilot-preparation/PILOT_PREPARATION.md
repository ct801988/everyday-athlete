## Recommendation and acquisition comparison
**Commission a small, purpose-shot two-exercise pilot with a movement reviewer present**, then evaluate the results before a larger library. This offers direct control of a consistent 3-frame bench and multi-angle hip CAR. If that budget is premature, use an owner-produced photo pilot with the same independent technique review and rights records. Do not buy a stock subscription before confirming matching sequences and required rights. These are options for approval, not spending authorization.

## Rights evidence before acquisition or use
Maintain a private rights packet, with public manifest references only: identified photographer/rightsholder and genuine original-photo provenance; signed agreement or exact asset IDs/license version plus invoice; permitted commercial app/web/paid-platform use, regions/term/user limits, resizing/cropping, offline device caching, contractors, backups and distribution; attribution text/location and expiry/removal obligations. Obtain signed participant releases covering the intended commercial context and location/property permissions where relevant. Camera metadata alone does not prove rights. Keep releases, personal information and contracts OUT of the public repository.

The U.S. Copyright Office identifies the photographer as the usual initial owner, with limited work-for-hire exceptions. ASMP's commercial agreement example treats the usage grant and releases as explicit contractual items. Commissioning is therefore not automatic ownership; request either an adequate written license or assignment with the agreed scope. A model release is not a substitute for the photographer's grant, nor does a copyright license establish correct exercise technique.

**Public GitHub/source ZIP gate:** Adobe's licensing summary restricts standalone-file distribution, including under its Extended license. A generic commercial-use license is not proof that stock originals may be committed to this public source repository or redistributed in downloadable candidate ZIPs. Require written compatibility with EA's actual delivery model. If incompatible, select differently licensed media or obtain separate approval for an asset-delivery/storage change; do not quietly alter hosting, repository visibility or architecture. No editorial-only or AI-generated substitute should be presented as real exercise photography. Check any attribution and participant-release requirements per provider/contract rather than assuming credits are always required or never required.
