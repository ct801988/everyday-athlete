# Pilot photo storyboards — review only
No photographs have been generated, acquired or integrated. All captions/positions below are draft shot briefs, not approved production instruction or a workout prescription. A qualified movement reviewer must approve the exact performance, range, angles and image-caption pairs before capture/selection is finalized. No sets/reps/progression are proposed.

Bench: initial three-frame teaching proposal; extra setup/unrack/rerack frames are allowed if necessary. Hip CAR: eight positions for one supported forward circle, not a disguised eight-frame cap. Additional angles and a separately reviewed reverse sequence can increase the count; never mirror, reorder or interpolate photos automatically to invent another side/direction. The minimum production count is three; no maximum.

## Flat Barbell Bench Press

Flat barbell bench, bilateral, flat bench/rack

| Frame | Position / shot | Draft short caption | Reviewer / camera check |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Setup / upper position | Plant your feet and steady the bar. | Side three-quarter; whole body, bench, rack and bar visible. Reviewer verifies stable body contact, grip, rack safety and the photographed bar position. Unrack/rerack may need extra frames. |
| B2 | Controlled lower position | Lower the bar with control. | Same camera, subject, grip, equipment and scale. Reviewer defines and verifies the appropriate lower position and elbow/wrist alignment. Do not force a generic depth. |
| B3 | Return / completion | Press back up while staying braced. | Same locked camera and full-body framing. Reviewer checks completion and body position; add setup/rerack views if three frames do not teach the movement clearly. |

## Supported Hip CAR

Standing supported right-hip CAR, one forward circle; exact range/angles pending review

| Frame | Position / shot | Draft short caption | Reviewer / camera check |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Supported neutral stance | Hold support. Stand tall. | Front three-quarter; full torso, pelvis, support and both feet. Proposed variant: standing, supported, right hip; stable support stays in the same place in every frame. |
| H2 | Forward knee lift | Lift your knee without leaning. | Same front three-quarter view. Capture hip flexion without substituting trunk rounding. Reviewer sets knee angle/range. |
| H3 | Begin outward arc | Guide your knee out to the side. | Same camera and body orientation. Show an intermediate transition, not a change of exercise or a 90/90 floor switch. |
| H4 | Lateral position | Keep your pelvis level. | Same camera; add synchronized second angle only if necessary. Capture the knee outward while the trunk and support remain consistent; reviewer determines the endpoint. |
| H5 | Rotation transition | Rotate at your hip; keep your body still. | Secondary rear three-quarter view if needed; clearly identify angle change. Show the internal-rotation transition and foot/knee relationship without implying that the knee joint performs the rotation. |
| H6 | Posterior arc | Move your bent leg behind you. | Rear/side three-quarter showing hip and trunk together. Reviewer checks that the pictured motion comes from the hip rather than back arching or pelvic twisting. |
| H7 | Return toward neutral | Lower your leg with control. | Maintain the reviewed view; include stance foot and pelvis. Capture the transition under the hip, not a jump between disconnected poses. |
| H8 | Finish / reset | Return to your starting position. | Return to the original front three-quarter view. Completes one forward circle only. A reverse-direction demonstration requires separately reviewed ordering/captions and extra frames as needed. |
